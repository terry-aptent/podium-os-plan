Podium OS

An operating system for the back office of Olympic sport — prepared as a working concept for Jack Gierhart, CEO, US Biathlon.

US Biathlon runs on roughly $3.7M a year and about a dozen staff who do the work of an HR department, a compliance office, a development shop, and a sponsorship agency — one person at a time, mostly in spreadsheets. That pattern repeats across almost every National Governing Body that isn't Swimming, Hockey, or Track & Field, and again, smaller, across the clubs and associations beneath them.

50
U.S. National Governing Bodies recognized by the USOPC — the large majority run budgets and staffing in US Biathlon's range, each independently reinventing the same board decks, sponsor reports, and compliance trackers.
01 · The opportunity

Compliance tracking, membership admin, donor and sponsor relationship management, sponsor ROI reporting, and board/USOPC reporting have no dedicated systems at most NGBs and no budget to build any. Large language models make the judgment-heavy parts of this work — drafting a sponsor report, matching a grant to eligibility, flagging who's about to lapse on SafeSport — newly automatable by a small vertical SaaS team, which wasn't true five years ago. The incumbent tools in this space were built registration-first, pre-AI, and haven't caught up.

02 · What it does

One platform, modular enough that a small NGB can adopt it one piece at a time — starting with the piece that hurts most.

01

Compliance hub

SafeSport training & background-check status per person, expiring-document alerts, audit-ready reports for the USOPC.

02

Membership & athletes

Registration, eligibility, licensing, results and ranking integration.

03

Development CRM

Donor pipeline, major-gift tracking, AI grant-matching, campaign management.

04

Sponsorship

Sponsor CRM, deliverable tracking, AI-drafted impact/ROI reports, renewal pipeline.

05

Board & funder reporting

AI assembles every module into board decks, USOPC filings, and grant reports — review-and-send, not build-from-scratch.

06

People ops

Onboarding, volunteer & stipend tracking; integrates Gusto/Rippling rather than rebuilding payroll.

Deliberately not built: a payroll engine, a background-check vendor, a SafeSport LMS. Those are regulated and already mandated — Podium OS orchestrates on top of them, which also lowers the switching barrier to adopt.

04 · Why now, why not already solved
SportsEngine / Stack Sports
Embedded in several large NGBs for registration, eligibility, and background screening ($14–150/check). Strong on registration; no fundraising, sponsor reporting, or HR. Some NGBs have migrated away over cost and service issues.
General AMS
ClubExpress, Wild Apricot, GrowthZone — not sport-specific, no SafeSport compliance model, no sponsor or board-reporting workflow.
Nonprofit CRM / grants
Blackbaud, Bonterra ($1,500–8,500/yr for sub-$500K nonprofits), Submittable (~$399/mo+) — solve money-in only; nothing on compliance, membership, or sport-specific reporting.
The gap
Nobody combines SafeSport-grade compliance, membership, fundraising/sponsor CRM, AI-generated board reporting, and lightweight HR in one NGB-shaped product.
05 · Financial model

Two tiers: an NGB tier (full module set, high-touch sales, ~$1,500–5,000/mo) and a grassroots tier (self-serve, lighter feature set, ~$100–500/mo) that opens in Year 2 once the NGB product and sales motion are proven.

06 · Go-to-market
  • Beachhead: build with US Biathlon as design partner — discounted pilot for a reference case study, product feedback, and Jack Gierhart as an advisor/connector.
  • Wedge first: ship Compliance Hub + Board/Sponsor Reporting within ~90 days — the fastest path to a provable hours-saved, dollars-raised story.
  • Expand through the network: NGB CEOs are a tight ~50-person peer group with regular touchpoints (USOPC NGB Assembly, annual leadership conference) — CEO-to-CEO referral likely outperforms cold outbound.
  • Move down-market: self-serve grassroots tier once the NGB motion and product are proven; later, license to international federations.
